# Source data and interpretation

The case-specific CSVs retain short keyword-in-context passages required for the lexical sensitivity check. Source document filenames and document hashes have been replaced with case-local aliases; identical identifiers remain identical for duplicate checks. Aliases do not identify publications. Detailed source-to-alias provenance and licensed full-text are kept in restricted research storage. This repository does not reproduce the original full-corpus clustering or provide full-text publications.
