# Case 1 — semantic sensitivity interpretation

Two saved sentence-transformer models each scored 500 source-indexed CVC candidate contexts for compensation/mechanism versus generic strategic incentive anchors. all-MiniLM-L6-v2 preferred compensation in 331/500; paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2 preferred compensation in 363/500; binary agreement was 80.4%. Similarity scores and preference are descriptive—not probabilities, inference tests, independent source-level coding, or a recreation of the original DBSCAN clustering.

The evidence supports an incentive-preservation *candidate*, but does not formally qualify the one-to-one preservation signal. Original PDFs and OD mapping must be compared at practice level to distinguish actual managerial remuneration from generic corporate incentives, and independent publications must be counted without duplicate files. Avoid claiming the semantic test proves preservation.
